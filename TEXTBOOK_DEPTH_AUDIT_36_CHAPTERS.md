## What counts as an authentic university-style textbook chapter?

An internally complete chapter must offer an integrated argument with clear conceptual foundations and historical context, separate theory families, operational definitions and instruments, **named primary studies with actual research design and responsibly cited findings**, contradictory results and alternative mechanisms, clinical diagnostic differences (when relevant), cross-population limitations, critical synthesis and a coherent sequence from foundations to advanced implications. Related activities and case studies remain separate from the 250,000-word expository target. A word total alone does **not** certify college quality.

**Reference policy:** use independently authored explanation based on lawful external references, not copied textbook chapters; verify publication metadata and direct source-to-paragraph claims, especially where an online edition restricts reuse/AI ingestion. Noba, StatPearls, OpenStax and research links are external references, not automatic reuse permission.

## Mandatory editorial passes for every chapter

1. **Structural continuity:** replace fragmentary explanations with a sequence of linked arguments; the initial short sections can remain as previews until full chapters are edited into integrated reading.
2. **Foundational definitions:** distinguish measured traits, behavior patterns, inferred mechanisms and diagnoses; state why each belongs in a narcissism specialty.
3. **Theory history and alternatives:** present competing models without pretending that every explanation has equal evidence or that a model explains every observation.
4. **Study-level scholarship:** verify at least several directly relevant peer-reviewed sources where available, giving correct authors, date, sample, design, construct, findings and limitations; use no invented statistics or quotations.
5. **Methods and interpretation:** discuss confounding, longitudinal versus cross-sectional design, measurement overlap, generalizability, replication, selection and base rates as appropriate.
6. **Clinical and ethical boundaries:** do not infer NPD from perceived abuse, or abuse from NPD; distinguish treatment research from self-help, and risk assessment from diagnostic speculation.
7. **Integrative editing:** remove duplicated paragraphs and repeated generic warnings, improve transitions, and keep examples/cases separated from textbook-word count.
8. **Technical acceptance:** verify rendered reading, all 36 chapter navigation links, search/trait deep links, iPhone and desktop layout, preserved progress, and caching/update behavior.
